Title: Morning Routine - Bird Building

The morning routine for the Bird Building includes: check feeders, refill water, morning enrichment, quick health check of visible birds, clean front enclosure, record any injuries or unusual behavior.

Procedure Steps:
1. Arrive at 07:00 and unlock the front gate
2. Visual sweep from doorway - look for any obvious issues
3. Check water stations - all containers should be full and clean
4. Inspect feeders - refill if less than 50% full, note any moldy seeds
5. Perform one enrichment activity (rotate through: perches, foraging toys, water spray)
6. Observe all visible birds for 5+ minutes - note any abnormal behavior, injury, or distress
7. Clean front enclosure surface, sweep, pick up debris
8. Document findings in the zone log
9. Report any concerns to the coordinator

Safety Notes:
- If you see a loose wire near the back enclosure, flag immediately and note the exact location
- Do not enter the back enclosure alone
- Wear closed-toe shoes at all times
- Wash hands thoroughly before and after handling materials

Duration: Approximately 45 minutes to 1 hour

Last Updated: September 2026
Prepared by: Sanctuary Manager
